01 Days 1–5

Idea

The AI co-founder builds the plan from your skills, $100 budget, available time, constraints, and unfair advantages. You become its hands in the physical world—testing three candidate offers against real demand, not excitement.

Done meansThree offers, scored and ranked.
02 Days 6–12

Validate

Conduct ten customer conversations and put one clear offer on a landing page. The objective is not applause; it is learning whether the problem is urgent enough for someone to act.

Day 12: kill or commit. Keep the offer only when the evidence earns another ten days of work.
Done meansA committed offer—or a documented kill.
03 Days 13–22

Build

Turn the validated promise into an offer, a price, and the simplest viable delivery. Use AI to accelerate research, writing, workflows, and production. Keep humans in review. Do not polish what the market has not asked for.

Done meansA shippable offer a customer can understand and receive.
04 Days 23–30

Launch

Ask for the sale. Reach the first ten prospective customers, record objections, revise the pitch, and track the result in public. The market—not the co-founder—gets the final vote.

FAQ

Straight answers.

What the challenge is, what it costs, and what “AI co-founder” actually means.

What is HustleGPT?
HustleGPT is an AI co-founder experience for entrepreneurs. Its core system—the HustleGPT Method—moves an idea through validation, building, and launch on a 30-day clock. The focus is execution and evidence, not passively consuming information.
What is it not?
It is not a promise of easy money, a passive-income shortcut, or a substitute for your judgment. AI can accelerate the work, but you remain responsible for interviews, decisions, legality, quality, and delivery.
Who is it for?
It is for aspiring and active entrepreneurs who are willing to test a small offer in public, speak with customers, follow a disciplined process, and accept the market’s answer—even when the right answer is to stop.
What does it cost?
The $100 Challenge is free to join; the $100 is your maximum starting build budget, not a fee. The HustleGPT Playbook is planned at $39 and AI Agent Recipes at $97. Both prices are working assumptions and may change before release.
How does the $100 Challenge work?
You start with no more than $100 and move through four stages across 30 days: Idea (days 1–5), Validate (days 6–12), Build (days 13–22), and Launch (days 23–30). AI works as a co-founder; you do the human work; you build in public. Completion means documented revenue or a documented kill.
Do I need technical experience?
No. The challenge is about choosing and testing a useful offer, not proving you can code. Your offer may be a service, a simple digital product, or another legal business that fits the rules and budget.
Is HustleGPT affiliated with OpenAI?
No. HustleGPT is an independent brand. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by OpenAI.
How do I join?
Email hello@hustlegpt.com with the subject “I’m in for the $100 Challenge.” The challenge is free.
